# Notices

## nflverse data (CC BY 4.0)

The stats files (`docs/*-data.js`, `app/*-data.js`) are derived from the nflverse data releases:
https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data

- Play-by-play (nflfastR), depth charts, snap counts and player info by the nflverse project.
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Changes: regular-season plays were filtered and summed into player, coach and team-game seasons; rates, era grades (scores against each season's players) and twin scores are computed by this project; on 2003-2008 incompletions and interceptions with no receiver ID, the target was read from the play description and matched to that team's player.

These derived files are shared under the same CC BY 4.0 license.
